- **Sprint 2 Core Expansion (`F-06`, `F-07`, `F-08`)**: 100% implemented, integrated, and verified (**PASS**) with zero regressions against Sprint 1 baseline.
- **Sprint 3 Baseline (`F-09`, `F-10`)**: Formally contracted and planned (**PLANNED** / **IN PROGRESS CONTRACTED**).
